# Remixing a proven post

Goal: a ready-to-shoot script in the creator's voice, adapted from a post that already performs, on the board as a card.

The master rule: take the idea and the mechanics that make it perform, never the hook, the script or the topic verbatim. If the original's topic is outside the creator's world, translate the idea into their world.

1. **Pick the original:** list_top_content, or a URL from the user. Prefer posts that beat their own creator's usual numbers (viewsPerFollower).
2. **Watch it:** watch_video (the default watch is cached, so repeating it is free). Read hook, structure, scenes, editing, whyItWorks and reusableFormat.
3. **Read the creator:** get_creator_context - niche, audience, and above all the voice guide and samples.
4. **Find their version:** their topic, their story, their setting, something they can shoot this week. Name the mechanics you borrow: the hook device, the beat structure, the pacing, the visual trick.
5. **Write the script,** format "reel":
   - title; idea (2-4 sentences: the concept, and which mechanics it borrows); hook (the exact first line said or shown).
   - scenes, 4-10 in shooting order: setting, action, dialogue (real lines in their voice: short, concrete, how people talk on camera), speaker ("creator on camera" or "voiceover"), roll (A = on camera, B = overlay footage), onScreenText.
   - cta in their words, and a ready-to-post caption in their caption style.
   For a carousel original, write format "carousel" with slides (visual, headline, body), or build the real, editable carousel with the carousel skill.
6. **Save it:** create_card with the title, column "pre_production", the script, and sourcePost = the original's URL. One card per source post: repeating it updates that card.
7. **Hand it over:** give the user the card url and one line on what you borrowed and why it should work for them.

Write in the creator's audience language (niche.language in get_creator_context).

## On a free account

The board is on the paid plan, so create_card answers upgrade_required. Tell the user once that the paid plan would keep the script on their board and calendar, and give them the link. Then give the full script in the chat, in the same shape as step 5. The watch in step 2 counts against the 3 new watches a month unless the post was already watched.
